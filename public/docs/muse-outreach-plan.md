# SCAiL Community Outreach Plan (for Muse)

**Owner:** Joshua German · **Assistant:** Muse (Meta's personal AI agent) · **Drafted:** November 4, 2026

**Goal:** Book SCAiL's first free AI classes by getting real host commitments from libraries, schools, churches and community groups. Start in the Midlands, then grow outward.

**The ask every time is the same and small:** *a room, a date, and people who want to learn.* SCAiL brings the curriculum, the instructor and the equipment, and it's free to the host and attendees.

---

## 1. Ground rules for Muse (read first)

1. **Approval before sending.** For the first two weeks Muse drafts and Joshua approves every message. After that, Joshua may let Muse send follow-ups on its own; first contacts still need approval.
2. **Verify every contact.** Get names, titles and emails only from the organization's own website or a public directory. Never guess an email address. If no contact can be found, use the organization's general contact form or main line, and note it.
3. **One person, one email.** Write individually to each person. No mass blasts and no BCC lists.
4. **Say only what's true.** SCAiL is a new South Carolina nonprofit with a federal EIN. **Do not** say it is a 501(c)(3), that gifts are tax-deductible, that classes have already been taught, or give attendance numbers. Don't promise dates SCAiL hasn't confirmed.
5. **Respect a no.** One first message, up to two follow-ups (day 5, day 12), then stop. If anyone asks not to be contacted, stop immediately and log it.
6. **Log everything** in the SCAiL tracker (Contacts and Calendar): who, when, what was sent, the reply, and the next step.
7. **Hand off to Joshua** any reply that is a yes, a request for a call, a question about money or legal status, or anything sensitive.

**Before sending anything:** set up the SCAiL email address (see section 6). Until then, send from Joshua's personal email with the signature in section 5.

## 4. When someone says yes

1. Muse thanks them and proposes two or three date options (weekday evenings or Saturday mornings) and asks about the room: size, Wi-Fi, a projector or TV.
2. Muse creates a calendar hold and logs the host in the tracker.
3. Muse hands the thread to Joshua to confirm the class, choosing from AI 101, AI Safety, AI for Your Job or Youth AI Lab.
4. After the class: a thank-you within 48 hours, a request for a short quote or photo with permission, and an offer of a second session.
